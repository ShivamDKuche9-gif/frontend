Administrative Realities

The Challenge We Are Solving

Large-scale linear infrastructure projects often experience execution bottlenecks due to siloed paperwork, disparate state laws, and delayed field validation.

Fragmented Data Silos

Project proposals, circle rates, revenue maps, and court litigations reside across disconnected state and district archives without a unified master registry.

Manual Scrutiny Delays

Physical movement of Joint Measurement Survey (JMS) records, gazette notifications, and objections extends statutory approval turnaround by months.

Limited Visibility into Possession

Authorities struggle to confirm whether compensation disbursement has translated to actual physical possession without field disputes or encroachments.

Inconsistent R&R Monitoring

Statutory Rehabilitation & Resettlement (R&R) compliance for displaced and affected families lacks longitudinal tracking once initial awards are declared.

Statutory Timeline Lapses

Strict deadlines governed by Section 25 of the RFCTLARR Act (statutory award lapsing after notification) are difficult to monitor across hundreds of active packages.

Absence of Unified Analytics

National decision-makers lack macro-level comparative intelligence on total acreage notified, acquired, solatium disbursed, and possession roadblocks.

National Outcome

What Changes With a Unified System?

Transforming land governance from an adversarial, document-heavy friction point into an efficient, citizen-centric enabler of national infrastructure.

End-to-End Transparency

A single consolidated digital trail of every statutory notice, objection hearing, and award inquiry visible to all accredited oversight tiers.

Strict Accountability

Timestamped officer actions and immutable audit logging eliminate procedural backdating and administrative ambiguity.

Operational Efficiency

Automated circles rate calculations and digital shapefile validation reduce dependencies on manual verification loops.

Inter-Agency Coordination

Central ministries, state revenue departments, and district collectors share the same authoritative progress metrics simultaneously.

Real-Time Milestone Monitoring

Automated countdown alerts identify statutory lapsing vulnerabilities under Section 25 before awards are legally endangered.

Proactive Decision Support

Predictive anomaly detection highlights packages suffering from disproportionate objection rates or compensation bottlenecks.

Built for Nationwide Rollout

The multi-tenant system architecture is conceived to scale across all 36 States/UTs, accommodating over 740 revenue districts and millions of cadastral land parcels without database contention.
