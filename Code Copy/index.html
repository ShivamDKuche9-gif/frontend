Welcome to the National Land Acquisition & Management System—a unified digital platform developed for the Department of Land Resources (DoLR), Ministry of Rural Development. Designed to modernize the entire land acquisition lifecycle under the RFCTLARR Act, the platform seamlessly connects Central Ministries, State Governments, District Authorities, and Project Implementing Agencies from initial proposal submission to final land possession.

Help & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Find direct answers to common questions regarding regulatory procedures, portal workflows, and digital documentation.

This web-based platform digitizes the entire land acquisition lifecycle, completely replacing fragmented manual systems and state-specific processes.
It covers every phase from the initial project proposal submission down to the final physical possession of the land.
By providing standardized workflows, the system enables seamless coordination among Central Ministries, State Governments, District Authorities, and Project Implementing Agencies. Users can manage online submissions, digital scrutiny, document approvals, automated workflow routing, and comprehensive status tracking at every operational stage.

The platform strictly aligns with the statutory requirements of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.
It features automated tools to monitor real-time data regarding compensation assessed and disbursed.
The system actively tracks Rehabilitation and Resettlement (R&R) progress and logs the exact number of affected and displaced families.
Decision-makers receive customized MIS reports and executive dashboards that monitor timeline adherence, notifications issued, and awards declared to prevent project delays.

The platform utilizes advanced GIS technology for the geo-tagging of acquired land parcels.
This enables the spatial visualization of project locations on interactive national maps, cleanly displaying the area notified versus the area ultimately acquired.
To prevent overlaps and double-compensation, the system integrates seamlessly via API with existing land records, cadastral maps, and relevant government portals.

The primary users are government stakeholders, including Central Ministries, State Governments, District Authorities, and Project Implementing Agencies.
The website uses secure role-based access control, meaning different officials only see and approve the specific data relevant to their department or jurisdiction.
It also features a mobile-responsive interface so field officers can collect and verify data directly on the ground.
